What will I receive?

A three-day email journey with simple practices in breathing, meditation, and gentle movement. Follow the guidance in each email, from the comfort of your own space.

Is the Mini Reset really free?

Yes. The three-day series is a free offering from Amora School of Love. There is no payment required to join.

Do I need experience or special equipment?

No prior experience is needed. Find a comfortable space and give yourself a few quiet minutes. Follow your own pace and adapt or skip anything that doesn’t feel right for you.

How do the emails work?

Sign up with your email address using the form above. If Amora sends a confirmation message, confirm your address to begin. The three practices arrive by email over three days. Check your spam or promotions folder if you don’t see the first message. You can unsubscribe using the link in any email.

What if I’m already very busy?

This is designed as a small pause, rather than another task. Each practice takes just a few minutes. You can return to the emails whenever it suits you.
